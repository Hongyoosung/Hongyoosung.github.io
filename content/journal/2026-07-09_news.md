### A Definition and Roadmap for World Models
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06401

At the heart of advanced AI systems, especially those interacting with dynamic environments, lies the concept of "world models." This perspective article offers a much-needed scientific definition for these internal simulators, characterizing them as systems capable of learning and predicting an environment's structure and dynamics. By addressing the current ambiguity across AI subfields, the paper meticulously details key technical aspects and proposes a structured roadmap for their effective development.

**Technical Significance:** This work is paramount for engineering as it standardizes a fundamental, yet often vaguely used, concept critical for progressing AI in areas such as model-based reinforcement learning, embodied robotics, and physical AI. It provides a common language and a structured framework, essential for systematic design, rigorous evaluation, and collaborative efforts in building robust world models.

**Practical Application:** Engineers can directly leverage this paper's definition and roadmap to guide the design and implementation of world models in their projects. This ensures a more consistent and robust approach, leading to more effective autonomous agents in robotics, improved predictive models for simulations, and more efficient model-based reinforcement learning algorithms, by providing clear guidelines on their construction and prediction goals.

### FootsiesGym: A Fighting Game Benchmark for Two-Player Zero-Sum Imperfect-Information Games
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06514

Developing AI agents capable of mastering complex strategic interactions is a significant challenge. FootsiesGym emerges as an open-source, vectorized simulation environment built upon the minimalist 2D fighting game, Footsies. It serves as an invaluable benchmark for two-player, zero-sum, imperfect-information games, specifically isolating the cyclic strategic interactions of "neutral play" for efficient analysis. Its design facilitates high-throughput reinforcement learning training on standard hardware, making advanced research more accessible.

**Technical Significance:** This platform provides a standardized, efficient, and reproducible environment for developing and evaluating AI agents in complex strategic settings. The vectorized simulator dramatically lowers the computational barrier, empowering engineers to more readily research and benchmark algorithms for dynamic decision-making under uncertainty and adversarial conditions.

**Practical Application:** Engineers can utilize FootsiesGym as a robust testbed to develop AI agents capable of handling real-world scenarios characterized by adversarial interactions, limited information, and non-transitive strategies. This extends its applicability to domains like cybersecurity, competitive financial trading, or multi-agent autonomous systems, enabling systematic evaluation of agent adaptability and resilience in competitive environments.

### DreamPartGen: Semantically Grounded Part-Level 3D Generation via Collaborative Latent Denoising
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19216

The promise of text-to-3D generation is immense, and DreamPartGen takes a significant leap forward by explicitly modeling objects as compositions of semantically meaningful parts. This innovative framework uses Duplex Part Latents (DPLs) to jointly represent each part's geometry and appearance, alongside Relational Semantic Latents (RSLs) to capture language-derived inter-part dependencies. A synchronized co-denoising process then enforces mutual geometric and semantic consistency, resulting in coherent, interpretable, and text-aligned 3D synthesis.

**Technical Significance:** DreamPartGen significantly advances text-to-3D generation by incorporating deep semantic grounding and modeling inter-part relationships, moving beyond prior geometry-focused approaches. It provides engineers with enhanced control and interpretability over generated 3D assets, allowing for the precise synthesis of objects that adhere to complex textual descriptions at a functional and structural part level. This leads to state-of-the-art performance in both geometric fidelity and text-shape alignment.

**Practical Application:** Engineers can apply DreamPartGen in rapid prototyping and design workflows, generating complex 3D models for product development, architectural visualization, or game asset creation directly from detailed semantic descriptions. It enables the creation of highly customizable and functionally specific 3D content by allowing precise control over individual components and their relationships. This could streamline asset creation for virtual reality, simulation environments, and advanced manufacturing design processes.

### Rendering-Aware Bayesian 3D Gaussian Splatting with Native Uncertainty and Adaptive Complexity Control
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05522

While 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has shown impressive results in scene reconstruction, it typically lacks native uncertainty quantification and principled complexity control. This paper introduces a rendering-aware Bayesian 3DGS framework that addresses these limitations. It robustly tracks Gaussian geometry using a Normal-Inverse-Wishart (NIW) posterior over means and covariances, leveraging renderer-derived surrogate summaries. By re-rendering samples from this posterior, the framework natively provides predictive uncertainty, which can be extended with a Dirichlet-process for probabilistic component usage.

**Technical Significance:** Standard 3DGS's reliance on point estimates and heuristics limits its reliability, especially in scenarios with sparse data. This Bayesian approach provides robust, native predictive uncertainty for 3D reconstructions, which is crucial for error quantification and decision-making. It significantly improves active view selection performance (e.g., +0.453 dB PSNR) and reduces uncertainty coverage error (17x lower) while maintaining competitive training costs, offering a more robust and statistically sound scene representation.

**Practical Application:** This framework is particularly valuable for decision-facing tasks where quantifying the uncertainty of 3D reconstructions is critical. Its primary application demonstrated is in active view selection, allowing for more intelligent and efficient data acquisition in scenarios with sparse views or fixed budgets, such as robotic exploration, autonomous navigation, or industrial inspection systems needing to optimize sensor placement. It can also be used for reliable interval calibration, providing engineers with confidence bounds for the generated 3D geometry.
