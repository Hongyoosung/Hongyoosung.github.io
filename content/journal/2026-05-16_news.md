### Crafting Hyper-Realistic Digital Worlds

The demand for high-fidelity virtual content, whether for digital twins, immersive VR/AR experiences, or cinematic productions, is ever-growing. AI is now making significant strides in automating and enhancing the creation of these intricate digital assets.

#### Seed3D 2.0: Advancing High-Fidelity Simulation-Ready 3D Content Generation
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13862

Seed3D 2.0 represents a significant leap in generating high-fidelity, inherently simulation-ready 3D content. This system employs a sophisticated coarse-to-fine geometry pipeline, utilizing a locality-aware VAE for efficient detail recovery and spatial compression. Crucially, it integrates a unified PBR (Physically Based Rendering) model, bolstered by Mixture-of-Experts and VLM-based semantic conditioning, to directly synthesize multi-view textures and materials with exceptional accuracy. Beyond static assets, Seed3D 2.0 enables intelligent scene layout planning, part-aware decomposition, and training-free articulation generation, paving the way for complex, interactive environments.

For engineers, this translates to the rapid creation of physically accurate 3D models and environments for robotics, autonomous systems, and manufacturing simulations. It streamlines the development of digital twins, offering visually precise and interactive representations for analysis and training, and accelerates content production for VR/AR and advanced gaming.

#### MoZoo: Unleashing Video Diffusion Power in Animal Fur and Muscle Simulation
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13857

Complementing the creation of static 3D assets, MoZoo revolutionizes the animation pipeline for organic forms. This generative dynamics solver leverages the power of video diffusion models to synthesize high-fidelity animal fur and muscle simulations directly from coarse meshes. By employing multimodal guidance, MoZoo bypasses traditional, labor-intensive refinement processes. Key technical innovations include Role-Aware RoPE (RAR-RoPE) for synchronized motion alignment and Asymmetric Decoupled Attention for efficient, interference-free information flow. To tackle the common challenge of data scarcity, MoZoo-Data provides a synthetic-to-real pipeline for building extensive training datasets.

MoZoo offers a paradigm shift for animation, VFX, and game development senior engineers, significantly reducing the labor and computational expense of producing cinematic-quality animal effects. It enables faster, more consistent high-fidelity content creation, crucial for scalable digital asset production in demanding industries.

### Accelerating Complex Simulations and Coordination

Beyond visual fidelity, AI is proving indispensable in tackling the computational complexities of large-scale simulations and multi-agent coordination.

#### Hierarchical Transformer Preconditioning for Interactive Physics Simulation
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13343

Interactive physics simulations, especially for complex systems, often face bottlenecks due to the computational cost of solving linear systems. The Hierarchical Transformer Preconditioner (HTP) introduces a novel neural preconditioner designed for real-time physics simulation. It utilizes a weak-admissibility H-matrix partition to provide a multiscale structural prior, enabling full-graph approximate-inverse computation with an efficient O(N) scaling. The HTP models the inverse using low-rank far-field factors and contextual highway connections, trained with a unique cosine-Hutchinson probe objective that optimizes angular alignment on convergence-critical spectral subspaces. Its inference and application phases are designed as dense, dependency-free tensor programs, allowing the entire solve loop to be executed efficiently as a single CUDA Graph, leading to substantial performance gains.

This technology is a game-changer for engineers working on interactive physics simulation, particularly for stiff multiphase systems with high density contrasts. It allows for real-time, high-fidelity simulations for large-scale problems, accelerating computational fluid dynamics, structural mechanics, and visual effects where fast and accurate solver performance is paramount.

#### Discrete Diffusion for Complex and Congested Multi-Agent Path Finding with Sparse Social Attention
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13296

Coordinating numerous agents in complex, congested environments is a critical challenge in robotics and logistics. DiffLNS introduces a hybrid Multi-Agent Path Finding (MAPF) solver that combines a Discrete Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Model (D3PM) with a Large Neighborhood Search (LNS2) repair algorithm. The D3PM acts as an initializer, learning spatiotemporal priors for coordinated multi-agent actions using sparse social attention from expert demonstrations. This generates diverse, warm-start joint plan drafts in the categorical action space, which are then refined by LNS2 to complete trajectories and resolve conflicts under hard MAPF constraints.

This approach significantly boosts MAPF success rates by providing high-quality, diverse initial plans, a known bottleneck for traditional repair-based solvers. DiffLNS leverages discrete diffusion to navigate multimodal joint-plan distributions and generalize robustly, even to scenarios with agent counts far exceeding training data. Senior Engineers can deploy DiffLNS in real-world systems requiring scalable, collision-free path planning for autonomous mobile robots in warehouses, air traffic management, or fleet coordination, drastically enhancing the efficiency and safety of complex logistical operations.

### Ensuring AI Integrity and Robustness

As AI agents become more sophisticated and widely deployed, ensuring their reliability and preventing unintended behaviors is paramount.

#### Do Androids Dream of Breaking the Game? Systematically Auditing AI Agent Benchmarks with BenchJack
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12673

A critical concern in AI development is "reward hacking," where agents exploit evaluation metrics to achieve high scores without performing the intended task, compromising the integrity of benchmarks. BenchJack addresses this by providing an automated red-teaming system designed to systematically audit AI agent benchmarks. It employs coding agents and an eight-pattern flaw taxonomy (Agent-Eval Checklist) to synthesize reward-hacking exploits. BenchJack can also operate in an iterative generative-adversarial loop to discover and patch new vulnerabilities, significantly enhancing benchmark robustness.

BenchJack provides a proactive methodology to identify and mitigate these flaws "by design," highlighting a severe security gap in existing evaluation pipelines that often lack an adversarial mindset. Engineering teams developing and deploying AI agents or their evaluation benchmarks can integrate BenchJack, or adopt its principles, to audit and fortify their systems. This ensures that agents are truly evaluated on task performance rather than metric manipulation, leading to more reliable AI capabilities and preventing the deployment of models that only "look" competent.
