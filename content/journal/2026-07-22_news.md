### ReactiveBFM: Reactive Closed-Loop Motion Planning Towards Universal Humanoid Whole-Body Control
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30362

The quest for truly agile and resilient humanoid robots capable of navigating unpredictable real-world scenarios has found a powerful ally in ReactiveBFM. This real-time, closed-loop planning-control framework transcends the limitations of predefined motions, fundamentally enhancing humanoid reactivity. Its core innovation lies in mitigating exposure bias through a scheduled prefix sampling curriculum, which compels the generative planner to learn error-recovery directly from imperfect physical states. This approach, combined with an asynchronous replanning mechanism and trajectory chunking, ensures spatio-temporally fluid and jitter-free execution, effectively reconciling latency mismatches.

ReactiveBFM stands out by addressing critical limitations of current Behavior Foundation Models (BFMs), offering robust whole-body coordination in dynamic environments where traditional open-loop methods falter. By allowing generative planners to effectively learn from and adapt to real-time discrepancies, it provides a robust solution for inherent tracking errors and latency issues in complex humanoid control. Engineers can leverage ReactiveBFM to develop more agile and resilient humanoids for dynamic manipulation, navigation in cluttered spaces, and zero-shot interaction with moving targets, unlocking unprecedented physical agility and reliability.

### NaP-Control: Navigating Diffusion Prior for Versatile and Fast Character Control
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20209

In the realm of character animation and control, NaP-Control introduces a groundbreaking approach by integrating reinforcement learning (RL) with a task-agnostic diffusion policy prior for physics-based character control. Unlike slower, iterative gradient-based guidance methods, NaP-Control uses RL to directly manipulate the latent noise of the diffusion prior, predicting task-optimized noise to steer character behavior. This innovative technique eliminates the need for iterative guidance during denoising, leading to fast, robust motion generation without compromising fidelity.

NaP-Control tackles a significant bottleneck in diffusion-based control policies: the slow inference caused by iterative test-time guidance. Its engineering significance lies in dramatically improving inference speed and robustness, making it ideal for high-throughput or real-time systems. Game developers can use NaP-Control to create highly responsive and natural-looking character animations for complex locomotion and interactive behaviors. It also finds strong application in robotics simulation for training physically plausible agents and in VR/AR systems where realistic, real-time avatar control under computational constraints is paramount.

### Text2Villa: Hierarchical Generation of 3D Indoor Environments with Physics-Aware Analysis-by-Synthesis
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17145

Generating realistic and physically plausible 3D indoor environments has long been a labor-intensive process, but Text2Villa offers a transformative solution. This hierarchical generative framework translates natural language into multi-story 3D indoor environments with an impressive degree of physical plausibility. At the macro level, it employs a fine-tuned autoregressive layout generator to create complex building foundations, including polygonal boundaries and vertical connectivity. For micro-level asset arrangement, an Affordance-driven Physical-Semantic Scene Graph (A-PSSG) abstracts physical affordances, guiding a physics-aware optimization loop that integrates geometric collision detection with Multimodal Large Language Model (MLLM) reasoning to prevent artifacts.

This work addresses critical limitations in 3D scene generation by enabling the creation of complex, multi-story structures far beyond simple single-room or rectilinear layouts. Its innovative integration of physics-aware analysis-by-synthesis, combining low-level geometric engines with high-level semantic reasoning from MLLMs, sets a new standard for generating geometrically sound and physically plausible environments. Text2Villa is poised to revolutionize game development by enabling rapid prototyping of complex levels, accelerate architectural visualization, and enhance VR/AR applications by creating interactive digital twins or immersive experiences from simple text prompts, significantly reducing design iterations and manual effort.

### TopoGS: Planar Reconstruction via Topology-aware 3D Gaussian Splatting
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16838

Accurate 3D reconstruction is fundamental for numerous professional applications, yet existing methods often struggle with fragmentation and geometric inconsistencies. TopoGS introduces a novel 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) framework that overcomes these issues by explicitly integrating both planar and topological constraints. It achieves this by extracting global 2D topological relationships from multi-view image segmentations and anchoring Gaussian primitives to these structural elements. This formulation enables the joint optimization of plane parameters, rendering fidelity, and topological adjacency, ensuring strict multi-view consistency and mitigating geometric misalignments.

TopoGS's ability to produce topologically sound and coherent 3D models from raw images is crucial for engineering, providing the precision needed for rigorous spatial analysis and professional design workflows. By generating connected and structured geometry, TopoGS offers a more robust and reliable foundation for downstream tasks requiring accurate geometric reasoning and high-fidelity scene understanding. This significantly improves the trustworthiness and usability of 3D reconstructions. Its practical applications span generating precise digital twins for Architecture, Engineering, and Construction (AEC), enhancing environmental perception in robotics and autonomous systems for robust navigation, and empowering professional design workflows, industrial inspection, and immersive VR/AR experiences.

### Fast VEM Fluid Simulation
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17725

Computational fluid dynamics (CFD) is vital for many engineering disciplines, but simulating complex fluid-boundary interactions remains a computational challenge. FastVEM is an efficient fluid simulation framework designed to robustly handle these complexities by integrating a Virtual Element Method (VEM) discretization for incompressibility and boundary conditions. It employs a novel convexity-preserving cut-cell strategy for body-fitted grid construction and a VEM polynomial-space Particle-in-Cell scheme for advection. The core of its efficiency stems from a new Galerkin geometric multigrid solver, featuring a diffusion-free prolongation operator, which specifically accelerates the computationally dominant pressure projection stage.

This research is highly significant for CFD, addressing the long-standing challenge of high computational cost and numerical instability when simulating fluid flow around complex boundaries. By achieving up to a 100x speedup in the pressure projection stage and enhancing robustness with challenging geometries, FastVEM enables high-fidelity simulations that were previously intractable or prohibitively expensive. This advancement allows for more detailed and accurate analysis, leading to improved design and optimization capabilities across various engineering disciplines. Senior Engineers can apply FastVEM in projects requiring high-fidelity fluid-structure interaction or detailed flow analysis around intricate geometries—such as optimizing aerodynamic profiles, enhancing hydrodynamic performance, or simulating complex flows in biomedical devices—significantly shortening simulation turnaround times and deepening understanding of fluid dynamics in critical applications.
