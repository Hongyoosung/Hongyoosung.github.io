## AdaMARP: An Adaptive Multi-Agent Interaction Framework for General Immersive Role-Playing
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11007

Traditional LLM-based role-playing systems often struggle with dynamic environmental modeling and orchestrating multiple characters coherently. AdaMARP emerges as an adaptive multi-agent framework specifically designed to enhance immersion and adaptability in these scenarios. At its core, AdaMARP introduces an innovative immersive message format that seamlessly interleaves [Thought], (Action), <Environment>, and Speech, allowing for richer, more context-aware interactions. Complementing this is an explicit Scene Manager that intelligently governs narrative flow through discrete actions like scene transitions and role introductions, all backed by rationales. Trained on specialized datasets—AdaRPSet for the Actor Model and AdaSMSet for orchestration—AdaMARP demonstrates remarkable efficiency. Its components, even when leveraging smaller models (e.g., an 8B actor and 14B Scene Manager), have shown the ability to outperform larger commercial LLMs like Claude Sonnet 4.5. This technical prowess offers a robust solution for dynamically managing multi-character interactions, scene transitions, and on-the-fly character introductions, which are persistent challenges in current systems. For engineers, AdaMARP provides a powerful blueprint for developing more dynamic and engaging AI-driven interactive narratives, advanced video game NPCs, realistic simulation environments, and intelligent virtual assistants capable of managing multiple personas with consistent narrative control.

## Two2Four: Generative Quadruped Puppeteering from Human Motion
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26108

Animating realistic quadruped locomotion has historically been a complex and labor-intensive task. The Two2Four framework presents a groundbreaking solution, leveraging a two-stage generative diffusion model trained exclusively on quadruped motion data. This innovative system automatically translates ordinary human motion into highly realistic quadruped movement, streamlining a crucial animation pipeline step. Its structured conditioning and inpainting strategy support a wide array of actions while enabling fine-grained control over specific elements like head movement and individual limb puppeteering. This represents a significant engineering advancement by automating a complex animation task with enhanced realism and controllability compared to existing methods. By utilizing data-driven generative models, Two2Four effectively mitigates the challenges and manual effort typically associated with traditional motion capture or intricate retargeting setups in virtual production and character design. Consequently, virtual production studios, game developers, and animation teams can directly apply this technology to efficiently create and puppeteer highly realistic animal characters using readily available human motion data, accelerating content creation and elevating the fidelity of virtual environments.

## AtlasLC: Fast Codec-Ready Compression of Object-Centric 3D Gaussian Splatting
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26525

The rise of 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) has revolutionized photorealistic 3D representation, but deploying these assets, especially for Extended Reality (XR) applications, faces hurdles like high preparation costs, codec compatibility, and decoding latency. AtlasLC introduces a source-free and training-free compression pipeline specifically for object-centric 3DGS assets, directly addressing these bottlenecks. It efficiently removes redundant Gaussians and organizes them for compression using local-competition pruning combined with deterministic atlas packing. This process employs a lightweight single-pass sort-based conditional transport as a shared coordinate backbone, operating directly on released Gaussian assets without needing original images or per-asset optimization. AtlasLC dramatically reduces atlas-preparation time (up to 25x) and end-to-end compression time (up to 5x), all while maintaining a favorable balance of payload size, decode latency, and runtime FPS. Its "codec-ready" and "deployment-aware" design directly supports scalable and efficient XR asset libraries. Engineers can integrate AtlasLC into their XR asset pipelines to efficiently compress individual 3D objects, enabling faster packaging, transmission, and instantiation of reusable 3D models in VR/AR applications or digital twin environments where rapid loading, low latency, and accurate depth/silhouette cues are paramount.

## Gemini Robotics 2 brings whole body intelligence to robots
**Link:** https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-brings-whole-body-intelligence-to-robots/

The dream of truly intelligent, adaptable robots is moving closer to reality with Gemini Robotics 2. This advancement introduces a sophisticated intelligence layer featuring Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models for nuanced whole-body and dexterous motor control, alongside an Embodied Reasoning (ER) model for robust task planning and environmental understanding. The system seamlessly translates complex visual and linguistic inputs into precise motor commands, enabling robots to reason through multi-step tasks and collaborate effectively. A key innovation is an efficient on-device VLA variant that allows for local execution and rapid adaptation to new robot embodiments. This framework marks a significant leap beyond pre-programmed or teleoperated robots, empowering systems to learn autonomously and adapt to unpredictable environments. It provides capabilities for complex whole-body control, fine dexterity, and multi-robot collaboration, with the crucial ability to rapidly transfer learned skills to entirely new robot hardware with minimal data. Engineers can leverage Gemini Robotics 2 to develop intelligent agents for complex physical tasks, from cleaning cluttered spaces to performing intricate manipulation. The system's remarkable adaptability to diverse robot hardware, such as different humanoid bodies or grippers, drastically cuts deployment time, facilitating faster integration into a wide range of industrial, service, and multi-robot applications.

## CaM-Wolf: Causal-Aware Multimodal Agents for Social Deduction Games
**Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26393

Social deduction games are a fascinating testbed for AI's ability to understand and navigate complex human social dynamics, often relying heavily on non-verbal cues. CaM-Wolf is an innovative AI agent designed for these games that uniquely integrates multimodal perception and generation. Unlike conventional text-based AI, CaM-Wolf processes video inputs from other players, employing a reinforcement learning-trained, causal-aware Reasoner to establish logical chains between observable behaviors and hidden roles. This allows the AI to infer intentions and strategies based on visual cues. The agent then presents itself through an animated avatar, combining its sophisticated visual input analysis with animated output for more natural interaction. This work addresses a critical limitation of current AI agents by incorporating multimodal interaction, which is fundamental to human social dynamics. By integrating visual cues and causal reasoning, CaM-Wolf significantly advances the development of more human-like AI agents capable of understanding and engaging in nuanced social interactions, leading to superior gameplay performance and enhanced human-AI interaction quality. Beyond games, the CaM-Wolf framework can be leveraged to develop more sophisticated, socially intelligent AI systems for various applications. This includes creating advanced virtual characters for training simulations, enhancing interactive entertainment experiences (e.g., NPCs in metaverse or VR environments), and enabling more effective human-AI collaboration in scenarios where understanding non-verbal cues and complex social dynamics from multimodal inputs is crucial.
